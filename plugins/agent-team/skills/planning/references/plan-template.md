# Plan template

Use the sections that help the task holder execute and check this slice; do not fill irrelevant sections just to complete the template.

```markdown
# Plan: {goal}

## Outcome and boundaries
{Intended result, scope, exclusions, constraints, and acceptance criteria.}

## Behavior and approach
- {Observable behavior or failure case}: {approach and affected sources or files}
- {Consequential alternative or compatibility effect, when applicable}

## Validation
- {Behavior}: {check and pass condition}

## Tasks
1. **{Action}** — {pending | in progress | done | blocked}
   - Result: {independently checkable outcome}
   - Target: {file, module, symbol, dataset, or other affected area}
   - Owner: {task holder or worker, when work is split}
   - Depends on: {task numbers, or none}
   - Key check: {validation check and result when run}
   - Blocker: {cause and what removes it, when blocked}

## Decisions and risks
- {Decision}: {confirmed or recommended but unconfirmed; owner and affected tasks}
- {Assumption or risk}: {evidence and impact}
- {Open question}: {recommendation and what depends on the answer}

## Evidence and review
- {Source or unverified premise}: {what it establishes or leaves open}
- {Independent challenge, when needed}: {result and disposition, or cause and open questions when not run or incomplete}

## Follow-up
- {Work outside this slice, or remaining gap}
```
